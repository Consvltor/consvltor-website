Mechas

AI agents built around the people who carry the deal.

CʘNSVLTʘR designs sets of AI agents around one key person: a deal lead, a head of finance, a commercial director, a general counsel. The person stays in control. The agents do the work that can be handed over, and the person works directly with them on the work that cannot.

Why
Why not automate the role

Full automation of complex work has been tried many times and has not held up. Leonardo drew a mechanical knight around 1495. Nothing suggests it was built or that it worked. The armour that did work was plate: it did not replace the knight, it made him stronger.

Current AI agents are in the same position. They handle some tasks reliably and others not at all, and which is which keeps shifting. Work that carries accountability, depends on unwritten context, or sets its own objectives still needs a person inside the loop. We expect that to stay true as the models improve.

The name comes from mecha fiction: a machine with a pilot inside. The pilot reviews some of its output at checkpoints and works with it in real time on the rest.

What
What we build

Each kit is designed for one role and one person. It sits behind a single control layer, the cockpit, from which the pilot runs the agents, sees what they are doing and steps in when needed.

First step

Exoskeleton

One or two agents on the tasks that slow a person down most. Light to set up and quick to learn.

Full kit

Mecha

A full set of agents under one cockpit, tuned to the role, including the heavier tasks that need closer supervision.

Later, kits for several people on the same team can share context. When a pilot leaves, the successor takes over the kit and its history instead of starting from nothing.

We build on existing platforms, and write only what the role needs on top of them. The part we are selling is the design: which tasks go to agents, which stay with the person, and how the two are kept aligned.

How
How we work with you

Design. We map the role with the person who holds it and decide what goes to agents, what stays with the pilot and what needs a checkpoint.

Customise. We adapt agents we have already built to your tools, documents and way of working.

Maintain. We adjust the kit as the work changes and as the models change, and we add agents when a new need appears.

This is for private equity funds, their portfolio companies, and the lawyers and M&A advisers who work with them.
